# Version 03 cinematic backdrop prompt

Built-in ImageGen was used to create the project-bound background source:

## Final prompt

Create an ultra-wide monochrome anime environmental panorama for a dramatic
camera pullback. Show an empty elevated city street or engineered roadway
stretching horizontally across the frame and ending in a dramatic sketched
cliff edge toward the far right, with a deep valley and enormous landscape
beyond.

Build a vast cinematic world from distant angular mountains, layered mist,
sparse futuristic civil-engineering structures, retaining walls, bridge
supports, utility poles and cables, clouds, and a faraway skyline. Keep the
exact centre of the street open and uncluttered so a separate standing
character sprite can be overlaid there.

Use sophisticated anime background art crossed with an architect's and civil
engineer's concept sketch: expressive black ink, graphite, charcoal, technical
construction lines, perspective guides, loose hatching, white paper grain,
selective grey washes, fine distant detail, and energetic hand-drawn
imperfections.

Compose it as a cinematic 21:9 panorama. A long, nearly straight horizontal
horizon or road-edge line runs around the vertical middle. A second grounded
street line provides a standing surface around 62% of the image height. Keep
the central 20% as open negative space and place the cliff edge away from the
centre.

Use black, charcoal, graphite grey, silver grey, off-white, and pure white
only. No colour tint. The mood is huge, quiet, contemplative, and crisp.
Background scenery only: no people, characters, animals, vehicles, text,
signage, logos, watermark, border, or crowded central foreground.
