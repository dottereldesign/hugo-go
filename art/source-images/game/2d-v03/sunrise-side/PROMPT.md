## Final prompt

Create exactly twelve complete, isolated, full-body side-profile poses of the
same Hugo character, facing screen-right, arranged in a strict four-column by
three-row grid. Pose order: neutral side stand, confident walk, fast sprint,
sprint launch, jump takeoff, jump tuck, level glide, steep dive, upward jet
boost, braking flare, landing crouch, and hero finish.

Lock Hugo's identity, fauxhawk, age, body proportions, Sunrise wingsuit,
orange/cream/gold palette, teal engineering details, and polished stylized game
art to the canonical reference. Replace only the footwear with the supplied
black-on-black sculpted basketball-shoe reference, adapted into the same art
style without logos. Preserve the compact twin heel outlets; flames appear
only in jet boost.

Use a perfectly flat `#FF00FF` chroma background. Use one equal cell per pose,
consistent scale, and at least ten percent clear padding. Nothing may cross a
cell boundary. No cropped hair, hands, wings, shoes, soles, or flames; no
missing or detached body parts; no text, labels, borders, watermark, shadows,
or background details.
