# Version 03 Sunrise side-profile extensions

Built with the built-in image-generation workflow using:

- `hugo-sunrise-side-poses-magenta.png` as the canonical Hugo identity,
  outfit, proportion, palette, and side-profile rendering reference; and
- `shoe-reference-n-g133-black.png` as the footwear-shape reference.

The accepted set adds 22 complete figures from two strict `4 columns × 3 rows`
generation sheets. Two off-angle reaction drawings from the second sheet were
intentionally excluded to preserve the screen-right side-profile rule.

## Parkour and traversal prompt

Create exactly twelve new, complete, isolated full-body poses of the same Hugo
character, all facing screen-right, arranged in a strict four-column by
three-row grid. Pose order: high-speed anime-style arms-back dash; low stealth
crouch; knee slide; ground slide; forward safety-roll entry; compact forward
somersault tuck; forward-roll exit; diagonal shoulder roll; speed vault; lazy
vault; Kong vault; and precision landing.

Lock Hugo’s identity, warm tan/peach skin, brown fauxhawk, age, body
proportions, polished stylized game rendering, burnt-orange/cream/gold Sunrise
wingsuit, teal engineering trim, yellow wing membranes, and black sculpted
shoes with compact heel outlets to the canonical reference. Use a perfectly
flat `#FF00FF` background, equal scale, at least ten percent clear padding, and
one complete figure per cell. No flames, props, obstacles, shadows, text,
labels, borders, logos, watermarks, cropping, detached limbs, or non-profile
views.

## Wall, flight, and personality prompt

Create exactly twelve new, complete, isolated full-body poses of the same Hugo
character, all facing screen-right, arranged in a strict four-column by
three-row grid. Pose order: wall-run stride; wall-climb reach; controlled wall
brace; comical full-speed wall collision; wall-splat reaction; dizzy rebound;
cheeky wall peek; guarded wall-charge sprint; shallow diagonal dive halfway
between the approved level glide and steep dive; airborne recovery curl;
friendly thumbs-up; and cheeky finger guns.

Lock the same identity, proportions, Sunrise outfit, footwear, and rendering
rules as the parkour sheet. All wall and ledge interactions use implied
surfaces—draw no wall, ledge, prop, motion line, or impact symbol. Use the same
flat `#FF00FF` background and complete-figure isolation rules.

## Shallow-dive correction prompt

Create one complete Hugo side-profile pose facing screen-right at the exact
visual midpoint between the approved horizontal level glide and steep dive:
approximately 15 degrees nose-down, with Hugo’s head clearly lower than his
shoes. Preserve the streamlined arms-back silhouette, identity, Sunrise
wingsuit, black footwear, and flat `#FF00FF` isolation rules.
