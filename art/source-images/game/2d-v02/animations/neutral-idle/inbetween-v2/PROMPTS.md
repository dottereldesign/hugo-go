# Neutral-idle targeted in-between prompts

Built-in ImageGen edit mode was used on three exact A / blank target / B boards.
Each board has three columns and four rows. Only column 2 is accepted into the
runtime sequence; regenerated endpoint columns are discarded.

Shared prompt contract:

- use the left and right production drawings in each row as exact temporal
  endpoints;
- draw one complete full-body midpoint in the blank centre cell only;
- preserve Outfit 03 identity, fauxhawk, child proportions, wingsuit
  construction, shoes, scale, and upright registration;
- exactly two arms, two hands, two legs, and two shoes;
- no text, grid, scenery, shadows, extra figures, overlap, or cropping;
- gum must be vivid grape purple (`#7C3AED` / `#8B5CF6`), not pink, grey, or
  background magenta; and
- keep untouched space flat `#ff00ff`.

## Board A

1. 50% weight shift → toe-lift anticipation.
2. 50% raised toe → grounded gum-chew stance.
3. 50% bubble size between medium and large.
4. 50% stunned blink → annoyed anticipation, arms still at sides.

## Board B

1. Hand/forearm halfway from side to face on a shoulder-elbow-wrist arc.
2. Hand halfway from approach to gum contact.
3. Fingers secure gum and begin the peel with a short elastic connection.
4. Hand, elbow, and gum stretch halfway between the peel endpoints.

## Board C

1. Gum has just detached and the raised hand recoils inward.
2. Gum inspection begins changing into an eye roll, with exactly two arms.
3. One-third recovery: raised fingers flick the gum away.
4. Two-thirds recovery: gum is gone and the moving arm lowers toward neutral.

Generated chroma sheets are preserved beside the inputs. The soft despilled
transparent sheets are production sources; grape pixels are restored from a
matching no-despill protected matte before extraction.
