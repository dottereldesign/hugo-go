# HUGO GO! collectible companion artwork

These source images are retained for a possible future collection feature. They are not part of the flight engine. Runtime-ready copies may be reintroduced only when the collection experience is designed.

All names, questions, answers, labels, and stats must remain editable HTML/CSS rather than baked into artwork.

## Shared direction

Premium polished 3D-painterly fantasy companion art for a children’s educational flight adventure. Use rounded friendly proportions, strong central silhouettes, and a sophisticated collectible finish. No card frame, text, letters, numerals, logos, trademarks, or watermarks.

## Verdant Webkeeper

An owl-bear forest companion in moss-and-leaf clothing, holding a glowing seed orb in a luminous clearing. Fine golden-green energy threads connect plants and distant woodland silhouettes.

## Fulcrum Forgeback

An armadillo-tortoise workshop engineer in rounded bronze-and-teal flight gear, holding a compact lever tool beside a friendly flying machine.

## Runequill Griffin

A young cobalt-and-violet griffin holding a luminous feather quill in an enchanted library garden with blank parchment and glowing sound ribbons.

## Sequence Sprite

A friendly translucent crystal sprite guiding repeating trails of colored orbs and shapes through a floating puzzle garden.

## Enchanted answer-side base

A reusable midnight-blue enchanted-vellum surface with sapphire inlay, restrained gold filigree, corner crystals, and a calm central area for overlaid copy.
